Before you ask

The questions procurement asks first.

Are the sports halls air-conditioned?

No. They are indoors and under roof. That means play continues through rain and out of direct sun. It does not mean the halls are climate-controlled, and we would rather you knew that before you arrived than after.

Is sport included in the rate?

Not today. Courts and programmes are quoted separately from hall and room rates. If you want a bundled per-head figure, ask and we will build one for your dates.

Can the halls be combined into one large room?

No. There are four separate halls off one shared lobby. A plenary plus parallel breakouts works well. A single divisible ballroom is not something this estate has.

What is the largest seated capacity?

Up to 600 seated for corporate formats. Higher figures published elsewhere are floating capacities for standing and circulating events, which is a different number and not the one a conference organiser needs.

Can our delegates use the pool?

The pool is an event facility available to bookings on request. It is not open access for staying guests, because there is no permanent lifeguard on duty.

How far is it, really?

About 20 kilometres from Hyderabad, at Moinabad. Tell us which office you are coming from and we will give you a drive time for your start hour rather than a best case.

Is there parking for a full-company event?

Five hundred spaces on site, with coach access.

What happens if it rains on an outdoor programme?

The sports halls and the convention halls are both indoors, so an outdoor programme moves under roof without leaving the estate or losing the afternoon.
